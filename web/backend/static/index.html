♥ Hearts — How to Play

Objective

Hearts is a trick-taking card game for 4 players. The goal is to avoid taking points. The player with the lowest score when someone reaches 100 wins.

Point Cards

Each ♥ = 1 point. Q♠ = 13 points. All other cards = 0 points.

Deal & Pass

All 52 cards are dealt evenly (13 each). Before play, you pass 3 cards: Left → Right → Across → Hold (no pass), rotating each round.

Playing

The player holding 2♣ leads the first trick. You must follow the led suit if possible; otherwise play any card. Hearts cannot be led until "broken" (someone discards a heart). On the first trick, you cannot play point cards (♥ or Q♠) unless you have no alternative.

Shooting the Moon

If one player takes all 26 points in a round, they score 0 and every other player scores 26 instead.

Game End

The game ends when any player reaches 100 points. The lowest score wins.

Controls

Click a card to play or select it. During the pass phase, click 3 cards then Submit. Press H or F1 for help.
